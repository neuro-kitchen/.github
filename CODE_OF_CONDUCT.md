# Code of Conduct

neuro-kitchen is a scientific open-source community. We want it to be welcoming to everyone,
whatever their background, career stage, or experience with code.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people outside your field
- Give constructive, specific feedback on code and ideas, not on people
- Credit others' work and ideas, as you would in a paper
- Assume good faith

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or academic setting

## Scope

This applies in all neuro-kitchen spaces: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any events or channels run by the project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/neuro-kitchen/.github/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban anyone
whose behavior breaks this code of conduct.
